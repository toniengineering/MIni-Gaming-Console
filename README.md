This is a mini handheld gaming console. It runs off of a esp32-wroom32E, has a 1.8 SPI display and has a joysitck with 6 buttons. I also included a speaker, it's a mini speaker that will run through a 
MAX98357A to help amplify the audio. A headphone jack is also included since I plan to also listen to music on this. Since the display has a mirco sd card you can play a wider selection of games and include music files. My 8 buttons are four for gaming and the other to for a pause and play button. I'm using the other two buttons for sound, one being volume up and the other being volume down. I am also including mounting holes to help with my 3d printable case.
