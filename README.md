This is a mini handheld gaming console. It runs off of a esp32-wroom32E, has a 1.8 SPI display and has a joysitck with 6 buttons. I also included a speaker, it's a mini speaker that will run through a MAX98357A to help amplify the audio.
A headphone jack is also included since I plan to also listen to music on this. Since the display has a mirco sd card you can play a wider selection of games and include music files. 
My 8 buttons are four for gaming and two other for a pause/play and power button. I'll be using a rotary encoder for sound, one way will be volume up and the other will be volume down. My other two buttons will be used with help music so a next and last buttons. I'm also making a 3d printable case that this will fit inside of.
